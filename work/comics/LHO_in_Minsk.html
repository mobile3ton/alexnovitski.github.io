COMICS

Lee Harvey Oswald in Minsk

Year — 2026 Medium — Digital Original — Digital Copies — Not printed

“Lee Harvey Oswald in Minsk” is a short comic inspired by a dream I had back when I worked the night shift at a "No Frills" discount supermarket. I’d start at midnight and finish at 8 a.m., restocking shelves before opening time. That job didn’t just give me the comic’s setting—it also showed me how much perfectly good food gets thrown away simply because the packaging is torn or crumpled.
