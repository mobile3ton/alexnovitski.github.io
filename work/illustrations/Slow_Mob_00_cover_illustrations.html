ILLUSTRATIONS

Slow Mob cover and illustrations

Year — 2021 Medium — pen and ink Originals — available Published by — Halijafy

TI worked on the illustrations for Denis Trusov’s book in August 2021. At the time, I was on holiday with my wife and child in a small village near Kielce in the Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship. I had full use of a barn with huge gates where I set up my studio. It was a real pleasure to draw there. The illustrations and the cover turned out well. Unfortunately, I had no say in the book's layout process.
