WATERCOLORS

Medusa

Year — 2021 Medium — Watercolor Original — in a private Art gallery Giclee Prints — available

Project description:
Temple of Medusa is a hardcover collection of works by thirty international artists, curated by Century Guild. The book celebrates the enduring power and symbolism of Medusa, exploring her mythological significance and contemporary interpretations. Each artist contributes their unique vision, resulting in a diverse and compelling anthology that honors Medusa’s legacy.

This illustration reinterprets Medusa not as a vanquished monster but as a sovereign presence — a being whose gaze embodies awareness rather than punishment. Drawing on the book’s curatorial vision of Medusa’s victory, the composition merges classical balance with contemporary intensity, evoking the moment she transcends myth to become icon.

Created for Temple of Medusa, a hardcover collection featuring thirty international artists, the piece contributes to a modern shrine honoring Medusa’s legacy — a testament to her enduring potency and the reclamation of her image as victorious rather than feared.
